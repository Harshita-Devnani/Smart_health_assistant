Building a Symptom-Based Diagnosis App with Flask and Machine Learning

In the age of technology, access to timely healthcare is more critical than ever. We embarked on a journey to develop a symptom-based diagnosis web application, leveraging Flask for the backend and a Decision Tree Classifier model to create a user-friendly platform.

The Problem

The project began with recognizing a common issue: people often experience symptoms and want quick answers. It can be challenging to differentiate between various diseases, especially when symptoms overlap. Our goal was to provide a convenient solution for users to input their symptoms and receive potential diagnoses.

The Solution

We developed a web app that allows users to enter a list of symptoms they are experiencing. The app then uses a pre-trained Decision Tree Classifier model to predict the most likely disease. Here's how it works:

  1. Symptom Input: Users enter their symptoms through a user-friendly interface that supports a wide range of symptoms.
  2. Machine Learning Model: We trained a Decision Tree Classifier using a dataset containing symptoms and corresponding diseases.
  3. Prediction: The app uses the model to predict the most likely disease, providing users with instant information.
  4. Additional Information: To enhance the user experience, the app also provides a description of the disease, recommended precautions, medications, dietary advice, and workout tips.

Key Features

  • User-Friendly Interface: An intuitive and easy-to-navigate interface ensures that users can input their symptoms without any hassle.
  • Accurate Predictions: Leveraging machine learning, our model provides accurate disease predictions based on the symptoms provided.
  • Comprehensive Information: The app offers a wealth of information about the predicted disease, including descriptions, precautions, medications, and lifestyle recommendations.
  • Educational Content: To empower users with knowledge, the app includes informative blog posts about various health topics.

Conclusion

Our Symptom-Based Disease Diagnosis Web App brings the power of machine learning and healthcare information to the fingertips of users. It serves as a valuable resource for individuals looking to gain insights into their health conditions quickly and conveniently. By combining technology and healthcare, we aim to make healthcare more accessible and user-centric, contributing to better health awareness and the well-being of individuals worldwide.
